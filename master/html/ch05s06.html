Exploring a Day of Service

The commercial speed of each mode is the distance its vehicles travelled divided by the time they were tracked, stops and layovers included. 

SELECT CASE r.route_type WHEN 0 THEN 'tram' WHEN 1 THEN 'metro' ELSE 'bus' END AS mode,
  count(*) AS runs, round(sum(length(trip)) / 1000) AS km,
  round((sum(length(trip)) / extract(epoch FROM sum(duration(trip))) * 3.6)::numeric, 1) AS kmh
FROM stib_vehicles v JOIN routes r USING (route_id)
GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 1;
--  mode  | runs  |  km   | kmh  
-- -------+-------+-------+------
--  bus   | 15151 | 82947 | 10.0
--  metro |  1453 | 14424 | 18.7
--  tram  |  7271 | 45021 | 11.1
-- (3 rows)

The metro is only observed at its stations, so its trajectory is the straight line between consecutive stations, which slightly underestimates its distance.

The slowest lines during the morning peak are obtained by restricting the trajectories to the period from 8:00 to 9:00 with the function atTime. The operator && keeps the runs whose bounding box overlaps the period, using the GiST index of the table. 

SELECT line_id, count(*) AS runs,
  round((sum(length(atTime(trip, p))) /
    extract(epoch FROM sum(duration(atTime(trip, p)))) * 3.6)::numeric, 1) AS kmh
FROM stib_vehicles,
  LATERAL (SELECT tstzspan '[2026-11-16 08:00, 2026-11-16 09:00)' AS p) AS s
WHERE trip && p
GROUP BY line_id HAVING count(*) >= 10
ORDER BY kmh LIMIT 5;
--  line_id | runs | kmh 
-- ---------+------+-----
--  T81     |   23 | 7.1
--  34      |   31 | 7.5
--  64      |   24 | 7.9
--  86      |   32 | 8.0
--  29      |   32 | 8.2
-- (5 rows)

The trajectory of one GTFS trip is the part of a run during which its temporal attribute trip_id had the trip as value. The operator ?= keeps the runs that ever carried the trip, and the function whenTrue returns the periods during which the trip was carried. 

SELECT vehicle_id,
  startTimestamp(whenTrue(trip_id #= '134460577341186002'::text))::time(0) AS since,
  round(length(atTime(trip, whenTrue(trip_id #= '134460577341186002'::text)))) AS metres,
  duration(whenTrue(trip_id #= '134460577341186002'::text))::interval(0) AS duration
FROM stib_vehicles
WHERE trip_id ?= '134460577341186002'::text
ORDER BY since;
--               vehicle_id              |  since   | metres | duration 
-- --------------------------------------+----------+--------+----------
--  022ff490-1234-4635-bf73-48f8003afa7a | 08:04:02 |   1845 | 00:10:28
--  b58377f3-6e03-4d18-ae0a-e1735a79ee9d | 08:14:30 |   5642 | 00:29:26
-- (2 rows)

The trip was carried by two runs: the platform lost the vehicle after 1.8 kilometres and found it again as a new run.

The time-weighted average of the delay, computed with the function twAvg, weighs each value by how long it held, and the function maxValue returns the largest delay of a run. 

SELECT line_id, round((avg(twAvg(delay)) / 60)::numeric, 1) AS avg_delay_min,
  max(maxValue(delay)) / 60 AS max_delay_min
FROM stib_vehicles WHERE delay IS NOT NULL
GROUP BY line_id ORDER BY avg_delay_min DESC LIMIT 5;
--  line_id | avg_delay_min | max_delay_min 
-- ---------+---------------+---------------
--  36      |           2.2 |            17
--  77      |           2.1 |            12
--  75      |           1.9 |            22
--  73      |           1.9 |            12
--  79      |           1.9 |            22
-- (5 rows)
